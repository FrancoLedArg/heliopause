Heliopause · Spec A · Fly a Hop · wireframe v2

v1 Screens

The player is with the Spacecraft, not looking down at a map. The main screen is the Spacecraft standing on the Body it landed on. Planning happens in a nav chart panel that opens over that view. A Hop plays out as the ship itself taking off, crossing space and landing.

Drawn at the game's native 640×360 with the real UI kit and the existing sprites, scaled up where a scene needs them big. Ground, ship size and particles are stand-ins for new art, listed at the bottom. Every number is an example, not tuned gameplay.

3 · Hop playback

After Launch the camera stays on the Spacecraft through all three Burns. The same HUD runs across them: the phase strip, a small route inset, the Fuel bar and the flight log. The Transfer is shown first because it is the longest part.

5 · Later: inside the Spacecraft

Not in v1. In later stages the player will be aboard during a Hop, fixing things as they go wrong. The flight view keeps the ship big and centred so a cut to the inside has somewhere to come from. Logged in the design backlog (#11).
